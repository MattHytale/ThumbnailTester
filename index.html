Install Thumbnail Lab on iPad

Open this site in Safari, then:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

Once installed, Thumbnail Lab opens full-screen and can work offline after the first successful visit.
